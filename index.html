The Architecture of an AI Recommendation

The practice of structuring your content, data, and digital footprint to be found, cited, and trusted by LLMs, not search spiders.

1. Entity Salience

Identity Assurance

LLMs do not see pages; they see entities. Salience is the confidence score a model assigns to your brand. We use Schema Injection to anchor your identity in the knowledge graph.

2. Vector Density

Information Gain

Information gain is the new ranking factor. We use Semantic Triplet Layering (Subject-Verb-Object) and Markdown Injection to create dense, fact-rich content.

3. Corroboration

Trust Signals

AI hallucinates. To prevent this, it seeks grounding. We build Citation Loops and digital PR footprints across high-authority sources to verify your claims.
